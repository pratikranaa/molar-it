Frequently asked questions

What teams and agents need to know.

Is Molar another test generator?

No. Molar executes your product in a real browser, verifies outcomes, captures evidence, and can turn proven flows into reusable tests. Generation is one output of the verification loop.

Can coding agents invoke Molar?

Yes. Molar is designed for agent-readable invocation through its CLI and MCP tools, with structured results that support a fix-and-verify loop.

Does Molar replace Playwright?

No. Molar builds on browser automation and can export Playwright. It adds planning, exploration, evidence, and verification around the deterministic browser layer.

How does it test Stripe, email, SMS, and storage safely?

Molar can use controlled clones and synthetic identities where configured, so teams can exercise integration behavior without destructive production side effects. Available coverage depends on the environment and configured services.

Can it test several roles and account states?

Molar has foundations for isolated sessions, persona-keyed accounts, concurrent scenarios, and bounded parallel exploration. The one-prompt Swarm orchestration shown above is a proposed experience built on those foundations.

Can Molar guarantee exhaustive coverage?

No system can truthfully guarantee every possible state. Molar expands a visible coverage frontier within explicit time, cost, and safety budgets, then reports uncovered branches, skipped work, and uncertainty instead of hiding them.

Can Molar run locally or inside a private network?

Molar is designed for local, shared-cloud, and controlled-network workflows. The right deployment depends on where your application, credentials, and evidence need to remain.
