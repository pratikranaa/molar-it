Molar Swarm · bounded persona exploration

One goal. Several isolated points of view.

Model who is using the product, what state they begin with, and what they are allowed to do—then preserve the coverage gaps and uncertainty alongside the passes.

Current foundation · persona inputs

A role is more than a different login.

Persona setup

New customer

Fresh account

No purchase history, first-run onboarding, narrow permissions, and a mobile browser viewport.

Returning buyer

Known state

Existing account, saved cart, previous transaction state, and a desktop browser viewport.

Workspace admin

Elevated authority

Billing ownership, member administration, and high-risk actions that may require approval.

Read-only member

Expected denial

Can inspect shared work but must be refused when attempting a protected mutation.

Current Molar accepts explicit personas and bounded combinations of role, account state, and browser viewport.

Boundary Browser viewport profiles are not native iOS or Android device execution.

Truth ledger

What exists now—and what this concept proposes.

Current Explicit personas, bounded pairwise population generation, persona-keyed coverage, named actors, and browser viewport profiles are implemented foundations.

Limited Isolated persona execution, concurrent exploration, shared coverage review, and ordered collaboration have code and local evidence, but hosted multi-persona proof and broad deployment remain open.

Proposed Describe a population once, launch the complete coordinated Swarm, and review every live session through this unified experience.

Proposed one-prompt orchestration

“Explore signup, checkout, billing, and permissions as a new customer, a returning buyer, a workspace admin, and a read-only member.”

This is a proposed experience built on current and limited foundations—not a claim of exhaustive coverage or universal hosted availability.
