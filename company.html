Software is built in pieces. People experience the whole.

Molar follows a browser action through your app and the payment, email, and identity services behind it.

Built by Pratik Rana.

Integration work at Wayground led to a question: why do we test screens separately when customers depend on systems working together?

Meet the founder

The screen can look right while a record is missing.

At Wayground, formerly Quizizz, Pratik worked on integrations with Blackboard, Canvas, Moodle, and Brightspace. A browser could look correct while a grade, roster, or assignment quietly failed to arrive.
