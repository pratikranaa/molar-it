MolarCompany

Software is built in pieces.
People experience the whole.

We’re building Molar to show whether a customer journey works from the first click to the final result.

The bug is often
between the systems.

Bengaluru, India

A user signs up. An identity provider redirects them. An email delivers a code. A payment processor confirms the charge. A webhook tells the app what happened.

Each system can look healthy on its own. The customer can still be stuck.

Molar grew from that problem: understanding whether the complete task finished, and making the conditions repeatable when it didn’t. A browser test, a service sandbox, and a collection of logs are useful pieces. Bringing them into the same workflow is the work.

We’re building browser execution, stateful test services, release checks, and trace investigation around one thing: the journey your user needs to complete.

Read the founding thesis

What we build around.

The customer’s outcome.

A click is an action. A confirmed order is an outcome. The test should know the difference.

Conditions you can repeat.

Reproduce the service state, the test identity, and the event that led to the failure.

Evidence you can follow.

Give a teammate or a coding agent enough context to understand what happened and check the next change.

that crosses systems?
