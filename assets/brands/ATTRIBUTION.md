# Brand asset attribution

These marks identify Molar integrations and accepted startup programs. They remain the property of their respective owners. Their presence does not claim endorsement, certification, investment, or partnership. Program marks should be displayed with the program names recorded in the manifest.

Complete provenance, license, and trademark notes are in [source-manifest.json](source-manifest.json). SVG files were checked for XML validity and for scripts or external resource references.

Seven catalog brands use official site favicons because a clean vector source could not be verified: Apify, Firecrawl, OwnerRez, PriceLabs, Ramp, Tavily, and Unipile. These raster assets are intentionally retained at their provider-supplied resolution.
