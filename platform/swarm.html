What should we bring to a Swarm preview?

Bring the application, the roles you need to test, and a task for each role. We’ll confirm the supported browser and account setup.

How many browsers can we run together?

Concurrency depends on the beta environment and workload. Contact us to agree on a supported setup before planning a larger test.

Can we see a multi-user example?

The team-invitation example shows separate admin and member browsers with Auth and Email Clones. It is a controlled scripted test; Swarm’s coordinated agent workflow remains in preview.
